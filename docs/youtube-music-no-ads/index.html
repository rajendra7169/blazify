Get Blazify
Free and open source

Listen to YouTube Music without ads, for free

Blazify is a free Android app that plays YouTube Music's catalogue with no adverts between songs. It keeps playing with the screen off, saves music for offline listening, and works without an account or a subscription.

Download Blazify free Get it on F-Droid

What you get without paying

No adverts

Songs follow songs. There is nothing to skip and no plan to buy to make the ads go away, because there are none.

Background and screen-off play

Lock the phone or switch to another app and the music keeps going, with controls on the lock screen.

Offline downloads

Download songs, albums and playlists and play them later without a connection.

Lyrics word by word

Time-synced lyrics from several sources, highlighted word by word, with translation and romanisation.

Gapless, with an equalizer

Live albums run on without a pause. A ten-band equalizer with 13 presets, bass boost and volume levelling shapes the sound.

Your library if you want it

Sign in with Google to bring your own YouTube Music library, playlists and likes. Everything else works signed out.

Start listening in a minute

  1. Install Blazify from F-Droid, where it is built from source, or from its GitHub page.
  2. Open it and search for a song, an artist or an album. It plays straight away, no sign-in needed.
  3. If you want your own playlists and likes, choose Sign in with Google. You can skip this step for good.

What to know first

Blazify is not on Google Play, so on Android you install it from F-Droid or GitHub. Premium has apps for televisions, watches and smart speakers; Blazify has none of those, only Android Auto in the car. The catalogue is YouTube Music's, so what you can find there is what you can play. Songs already on your phone play in the same queue as streamed ones.

Blazify is an independent project. It is not made by, or connected to, YouTube or Google.

Questions

Is Blazify really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no advert. It is open-source software under the GPL-3.0 licence, made by one developer, Rajendra Pandey, in Kathmandu.

Do I need a YouTube Music Premium subscription?

No. Blazify does not ask for one. You do not even need a Google account unless you want your own library inside the app.

Does it work on iPhone and computers?

Yes. Blazify also runs on iPhone, Windows and Linux. The iPhone app is installed with SideStore or AltStore, outside the App Store.

Is my listening tracked?

No. Blazify has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries of any kind.
