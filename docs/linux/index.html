Blazify for Linux

A music player for Linux that is free and open source

Blazify for Linux plays music from YouTube Music's catalogue on your desktop, with synced lyrics, your own playlists when you sign in, and no adverts. It is GPL-3.0 software, and it comes in the package format that suits your distribution.

Download for Linux Other devices

What it does on your desktop

Home and search

Start from the home page or search for a song, an album or an artist and play it at once.

A full player

Play, pause, skip and seek from a player view built around the album artwork.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that move with the song, so you can follow along while you work.

Video look

Turn it on and the song's own video plays behind the player instead of a still picture.

Look and feel settings

Adjust how the app looks so it sits well next to the rest of your desktop.

Your library, if you sign in

Sign in with Google to see your own YouTube Music library and playlists. Without it, search and play still work.

Pick your package

The audio engine is built into the app, so you do not have to set up a separate player first. All three packages are on the Blazify page linked above, which carries the current desktop version, 1.4.0.

What to know first

The desktop app is younger than the Android one, which has the longer feature list. It streams from YouTube Music's catalogue, so it needs an internet connection, and a song that is not on YouTube Music will not be in Blazify either. If you also listen on your phone, the Android and iPhone apps are linked from the Blazify home page.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no advert. Blazify is released under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Do I need an account?

No. Search and playback work signed out. Signing in with Google only adds your own YouTube Music library and playlists.

Is my listening tracked?

No. Blazify has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries of any kind.

Does it run anywhere else?

Yes. Blazify also runs on Windows, Android and iPhone. It is made by one developer, Rajendra Pandey, in Kathmandu, Nepal.
