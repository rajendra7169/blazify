Blazify for iPhone

A YouTube Music player for iPhone, without the App Store

Blazify for iPhone is free and open source. You install it yourself with SideStore or AltStore, then search, play and read synced lyrics with no adverts, and the music keeps playing when you leave the app.

Download for iPhone Other devices

What the iPhone app does

Search and play

Find songs, albums and artists in YouTube Music's catalogue and play them, with no adverts in between.

Library and playlists

Keep the songs you like and build playlists of your own.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that move with the song, so you can follow along or sing.

Background playback

Lock the phone or open another app and the music carries on.

Moods and genres

Browse by mood or genre when you do not know what you want to hear.

Player designs

Several looks for the player, a cassette among them, each with a live preview before you choose it.

Install it in four steps

Apps from outside the App Store are installed with a sideloading tool. SideStore and AltStore both work, and both sign the app with your own Apple ID.

  1. Install SideStore or AltStore on your iPhone, following the guide on its own website.
  2. On the iPhone, open the latest Blazify release on GitHub and download Blazify.ipa.
  3. Open the downloaded file with SideStore or AltStore. It installs Blazify on your home screen.
  4. Refresh the app once a week, as explained below.

The weekly refresh

With a free Apple ID, Apple lets a sideloaded app run for seven days before it has to be signed again. Refreshing takes a tap in SideStore or AltStore and keeps your library, playlists and settings as they are. SideStore can do the refresh on the phone itself, without a computer nearby. If a week passes and you forget, the app simply will not open until you refresh it.

What to know first

The iPhone app is at version 0.4.0 and is younger than the Android app, so it does fewer things today. It is not in the App Store, so updates come the same way as the first install: download the new release and open it with SideStore or AltStore. It streams from YouTube Music's catalogue, so a song that is not on YouTube Music will not be in Blazify either.

Questions

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?

No. SideStore and AltStore install apps signed with your own Apple ID. Nothing about the phone's system is changed.

Does it cost anything?

No. Blazify is free, with no paid plan and no advert. A free Apple ID is enough to install it; the only cost is the refresh every seven days.

Is my listening tracked?

No. Blazify has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries of any kind. The source code is public under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Who makes it?

One developer, Rajendra Pandey, in Kathmandu, Nepal. The same app also runs on Android, Windows and Linux.
