A free Spotify alternative, with no ads at all

Blazify plays the songs you choose, in the order you choose, without adverts between them. Save music for offline listening, read the lyrics word by word as they are sung, and use it without making an account.

Download Blazify free Get it on F-Droid

What changes when you switch

No adverts

Nothing plays between your songs except your songs. There are no ads to skip and no plan to buy to remove them.

Any song, on demand

Pick a song and it plays. No shuffle-only mode on your phone.

Offline downloads, free

Download songs, albums and playlists and listen without a connection, on the bus or on a plane.

Lyrics word by word

Time-synced lyrics that highlight each word as it is sung, from several sources, with translation.

A real equalizer

Ten bands with thirteen presets, plus bass boost and volume levelling between songs.

No account needed

Everything works signed out. Sign in with Google only if you want your own library and playlists.

Bring your Spotify playlists

You do not have to start again. Copy the share link of a Spotify playlist, paste it into Blazify, and the playlist is rebuilt with the same songs, ready to play and to download.

  1. In Spotify, open the playlist and choose Share › Copy link.
  2. In Blazify, open your library and choose Import from Spotify.
  3. Paste the link. Blazify finds each song and saves the playlist for you.

What to know before you switch

Blazify streams from YouTube Music's catalogue, which is very large but is not Spotify's, so a song that exists only on Spotify will not be there. It is not on Google Play: on Android you install it from F-Droid or from its GitHub page. Apart from Android Auto, there are no apps for televisions, watches or smart speakers.

Blazify is an independent project. It is not made by, or connected to, Spotify, YouTube or Google.

Questions

Is Blazify really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no advert. It is open-source software under the GPL-3.0 licence, made by one developer in Kathmandu.

Does it work on iPhone and computers?

Yes. Blazify runs on Android, iPhone, Windows and Linux. The iPhone app is installed with SideStore or AltStore, outside the App Store.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Search, play, download and lyrics all work signed out. Signing in with Google brings in your own YouTube Music library and playlists.

Is my listening tracked?

No. Blazify has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries of any kind.
