Where Blazify comes from

Blazify did not start from nothing. Its streaming core builds on InnerTune and on Metrolist, which grew out of it, and Blazify would not exist without the people who wrote and maintain those projects. Thank you to all of them. Because every one of these apps is GPL-3.0, that kind of building on each other's work is exactly what the licence allows, as long as the result stays open too.

What Blazify adds on top is its own design and features, and apps for iPhone, Windows and Linux. It is made by one developer, Rajendra Pandey, in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Which one to pick

Blazify

Pick it if you want the same player on Android, iPhone, Windows and Linux, or if you install apps from F-Droid, where it is built from source.

Metrolist

Pick it if a large community matters to you. It is active and has about 14,000 stars on GitHub.

OuterTune

Pick it if you want another actively maintained app, available from both F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid.

InnerTune

The project Metrolist grew from, and part of Blazify's base. It still installs, but there has been no release since September 2024.

ViMusic

Archived and read-only since July 2024. If you still use it, any of the active apps above is a safer choice.

Questions

Are these apps free?

All five are free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. Blazify has no paid plan, no advert, no analytics and no tracking libraries.

Which of them run on iPhone or a computer?

Of these five, only Blazify does. The other four are Android apps. Blazify for iPhone is installed with SideStore or AltStore, and the Windows and Linux apps come from the Blazify page on rajendrapandey.info.np.

Can I keep my YouTube Music library when I switch?

In Blazify, yes: sign in with Google and your library, playlists and likes come with you. Signing in is optional; everything else works without an account.

Is Blazify on Google Play?

No. On Android you get it from F-Droid or from its GitHub page.

Blazify is an independent project. It is not made by, or connected to, YouTube or Google, and it is not affiliated with Metrolist, OuterTune, InnerTune or ViMusic.
