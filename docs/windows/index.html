Get Blazify
Blazify for Windows

A free music player for Windows, with no ads

Blazify for Windows plays the songs you pick from YouTube Music's catalogue, shows the lyrics in time with the music, and brings your own playlists when you sign in. It costs nothing, shows no adverts and installs without an administrator password.

Download for Windows Other devices

What it does on your computer

A home page to start from

Open the app and there is music to play right away, or search for exactly what you want.

A full player

Play, pause, skip and seek from a player view built around the album artwork.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that move with the song while it plays, so you can follow along.

Video look

Turn it on and the song's own video plays behind the player instead of a still picture.

Look and feel settings

Change how the app looks until it suits your desk and your taste.

Your library, if you sign in

Sign in with Google to see your own YouTube Music library and playlists. Without it, search and play still work.

How to install it

  1. Open the Blazify page on rajendrapandey.info.np with the button above. It always has the current desktop version.
  2. Download the Windows installer and run it. It does not ask for an administrator password, so it works on computers where you are not the admin.
  3. Open Blazify and play something. The audio engine is built in, so there is nothing else to install.

The current desktop version is 1.4.0, released in October 2026.

What to know first

The desktop app is younger than the Android one, which has the longer feature list. It streams from YouTube Music's catalogue, so you need an internet connection, and a song that is not on YouTube Music will not be in Blazify either. Get the installer from the Blazify page linked above, which is kept up to date with each desktop release.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no advert. Blazify is open-source software under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Do I need an account?

No. Search and playback work signed out. Signing in with Google only adds your own YouTube Music library and playlists.

Is my listening tracked?

No. Blazify has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries of any kind.

Is there a version for my phone?

Yes. Blazify also runs on Android and iPhone, and on Linux computers. It is made by one developer, Rajendra Pandey, in Kathmandu, Nepal.
