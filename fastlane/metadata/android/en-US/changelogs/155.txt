The first song now starts in about three seconds. It was taking ten or
more, and often never started at all: every launch worked out from
scratch that the site's current player cannot be read here, which costs
megabytes of parsing, and the token the first request needs was being
warmed too late to be any help.

Music on your phone plays now. The player had only the two formats the
online catalogue uses, so MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV files had nothing
to read them with. Formats it cannot open are no longer listed at all.

Covers show up where they were blank, and a song with no artist tag takes
its name from the file.

Home no longer leaves a gap under a short shelf, the last row sits clear
of the mini player, and a refresh brings different songs rather than the
same ones rearranged.
