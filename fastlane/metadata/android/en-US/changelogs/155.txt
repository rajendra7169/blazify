The first song now starts in about three seconds. It was taking ten or
more and often never started at all, because every launch worked out from
scratch that the site's current player cannot be read here, and the token
the first request needs was warmed too late to help.

Music on your phone plays now. The player had only the two formats the
online catalogue uses, so MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV had nothing to
read them with. Covers show up where they were blank, a song with no
artist tag is given one, and a file that describes itself badly can be
told what it is. Formats the player cannot open are no longer listed.

Local music can be browsed by folder, and the shortest and smallest files
can be left out, so a voice note from a chat stops sitting next to the
albums.

Room invites go somewhere that exists. Menus no longer offer a local file
things it cannot do. Home leaves no gap under a short shelf, the last row
sits clear of the mini player, and a refresh brings different songs.
