Music on your phone plays now. It did not before: the player was only
given the two formats the online catalogue uses, so MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus
and WAV files had nothing to read them with and every one of them stopped
the moment it was asked to start.

Files in formats the player cannot open, such as Windows Media, are no
longer listed at all rather than listed and then refused.

Covers show up where they were blank, both beside each song and on the
library card, and a song with no artist tag now takes the name from the
file instead of leaving the line empty.

The last row of a list no longer sits flush against the mini player.
