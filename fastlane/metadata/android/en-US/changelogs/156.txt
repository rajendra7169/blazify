Fixes a crash. On some phones 9.13.2 closed itself as it opened, before you
could touch anything, and it could close again while a song was starting.

The cause: the catalogue does not always say how long a file is, and the app
insisted that it had. That happened on a background thread where nothing was
watching, so the app went down instead of losing one song's volume-levelling
figures.

Sixty-four pieces of background work ran with nothing to catch a failure. None
of them can close the app now.

Release notes inside the app read as sentences instead of arriving with their
own pipes and backticks.
