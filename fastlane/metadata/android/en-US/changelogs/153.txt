Blazify now plays the music already on your phone. Point it at the folders your
music lives in — it asks for the folders, not for the whole phone — and local
songs sit in the library beside everything else and play the same way.

Settings, About, Report a problem writes the version, the phone and the Android
release for you, and opens your mail app or the issue tracker. Nothing is sent
anywhere without you seeing it first.

Search opens on moods and genres to browse instead of an empty page under a
keyboard, and your recent searches now wrap so you can see all of them.

The player's bottom row shows what is on: queue, sleep timer and lyrics light up
while they are running. The pure black theme is properly black everywhere,
including cards and sheets, which used to keep a colour of their own.
